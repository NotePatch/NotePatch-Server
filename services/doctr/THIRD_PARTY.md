# DocTr

The source under `vendor/DocTr` is vendored from the DocTr project and remains
subject to its bundled `LICENSE.md`. Model weights are deliberately excluded
from this repository and must be placed under `${NOTEPATCH_DATA_ROOT}/models/doctr`.

The bundled license permits non-commercial use and requires attribution and
share-alike distribution of modifications. Commercial use requires explicit
prior written permission from the DocTr author. Review that license before
deploying NotePatch in a commercial product.
